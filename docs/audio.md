# Audio credits

EggScape bundles the following sounds as PCM WAV files for playback through Java Sound.

| Use | Bundled file | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Egg roulette | `wheel-spin.wav` | [Wheel Spin Click Slow Down](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/film-special-effects-wheel-spin-click-slow-down-101152/) by pooky1 (Freesound), published through freesound_community on Pixabay |
| Egg ready | `ready-horn.wav` | [Birthday Party Horn](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/film-special-effects-birthday-party-horn-250238/) by Universfield on Pixabay |
| Optional Jerry Sound Pack | `jerry-1.wav` through `jerry-5.wav` | Five recordings supplied by jerrypeter for inclusion in EggScape |

The two Pixabay source pages identify their sounds as available under the [Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/). The WAV conversions retain their respective source terms.

The Jerry recordings include the supplied trims. Conversion utilities are not required or included at runtime. The roulette sound can be muted separately from the hatch-ready sound, and the Jerry Sound Pack is optional.
